## 📊 Sales & Finance Analysis — MySQL SQL Project
### 📌 Project Overview

This Sales & Finance Analysis SQL Project uses MySQL to analyze transactional sales data and generate meaningful business insights. The project focuses on evaluating sales performance, revenue, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and sales territory contribution.

Using a structured fact-and-dimension data model, the project applies SQL techniques to transform raw transactional data into useful business information. The analysis answers real-world business questions such as identifying top-performing products, high-value customers, profitable products, and territories that contribute the most to overall company sales.

---

### 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

Analyze overall sales and financial performance.
Calculate key business KPIs such as revenue, profit, sales quantity, and profit margin.
Identify the top-performing and most profitable products.
Analyze customer purchasing behavior and revenue contribution.
Evaluate sales performance across different territories.
Calculate each territory's percentage contribution to total company sales.
Identify the highest-selling product within each territory.
Analyze sales trends across different time periods.
Rank products and customers based on business performance.
Apply advanced SQL techniques to solve practical business problems.
Generate insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

### The analysis provides several important business insights:

Sales performance varies significantly across territories, making territory-level analysis important for identifying high-performing and underperforming regions.
A small group of products contributes a significant portion of total sales, allowing businesses to prioritize high-performing products.
Product sales and product profitability are not always the same, demonstrating the importance of analyzing profit in addition to revenue.
High-value customers can be identified based on their total revenue and order frequency, providing opportunities for targeted customer retention strategies.
Territory contribution analysis reveals which regions have the greatest impact on overall company revenue.
Ranking products within each territory helps identify regional product preferences and demand patterns.
Sales trends over time provide visibility into changes in business performance and can help identify periods of increased or decreased demand.
Customer and product segmentation allows management to focus resources on the areas with the greatest business impact.
